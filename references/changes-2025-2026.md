# What changed

- **From Y/A 2025/26** (Amendment Act No. 2 of 2025): personal relief Rs. 1.2M → **Rs. 1.8M**; new slabs 1M @6% then 18/24/30/36%; rent relief 25%; solar relief Rs. 600,000; work-abroad residency rule; FCY service income capped at 15%.
- **Inland Revenue (Amendment) Act No. 11 of 2026** (certified 3 June 2026): CGT for individuals 10% → **15%** from 3 June 2026; tougher compliance (notice-then-prosecution for non-filing, fines up to Rs. 400,000); interest-write-off provision; no SET from Y/A 2026/27; simplified acceptance of returns where declared tax is ≥120% of the prior year and an affidavit is given (confirm applicability).
- **November Budget**: watch for changes to slabs, relief and WHT; announcements normally take effect from 1 April.
- No change to the Y/A 2026/27 relief or slabs had been announced as at October 2026.
