# Deadlines, instalments and penalties

## Calendar for Y/A starting 1 April of year N
| What | Due |
|---|---|
| Q1 instalment (Apr–Jun) | 15 Aug, N |
| Q2 instalment (Jul–Sep) | 15 Nov, N |
| Q3 instalment (Oct–Dec) | 15 Feb, N+1 |
| Q4 instalment (Jan–Mar) | 15 May, N+1 |
| Final payment | 30 Sep, N+1 |
| Return of income | 30 Nov, N+1 |
| CGT | within 30 days of the sale |
| Employer APIT / WHT remittance | 15th of following month |
| Refund claim | within 30 months of end of the Y/A |

Y/A 2025/26: final payment 30 Sep 2026, **return 30 Nov 2026**. Y/A 2026/27: instalments Aug 2026 → May 2027, return 30 Nov 2027.

## Instalments
From Y/A 2026/27 no Statement of Estimated Tax (SET). Each instalment ≈ ¼ of the previous year's tax; IRD-specified method if no tax last year or income is expected to fall. APIT-only employees pay none.

## Penalties (general — figures have changed in 2025–26 amendments; confirm exact amounts at ird.gov.lk)
- **Late return**: monetary penalty plus possible further assessment. IRD warns non-filers of fines up to Rs. 400,000 and up to six months' imprisonment after written notice (Amendment Act No. 11 of 2026; 30 days' notice to comply before prosecution).
- **Late payment**: interest on unpaid tax per month, plus penalties on shortfalls.
- **Understatement / false return**: percentage penalty on the tax understated; wilful evasion is an offence.
- **2026 interest waiver**: Commissioner-General may write off interest on certain underpayments/late payments if the principal tax and penalties are paid within the prescribed period (six months in the bill) — confirm eligibility and window.
