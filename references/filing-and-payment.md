# TIN, RAMIS e-filing, documents and payment

## 1. Get a TIN
- Register at **ird.gov.lk → RAMIS → Register** (needs NIC, mobile, email). A TIN is the same number for life. Registration is mandatory once you have taxable income, a business, or when asked by a bank/authority.
- The person creates their own RAMIS username/password — never share it with anyone, including this assistant.

## 2. Documents checklist
- NIC / passport, TIN.
- Employment: T-10 (APIT certificate) from each employer.
- Interest: bank AIT/WHT certificates. Rent: tenant WHT certificate, agreement. Service fees: WHT certificate.
- Business/freelance: income and expense records, bank statements, foreign remittance (FCY) proofs.
- Donation receipts (approved charity), solar invoice/loan statements.
- Instalment payment slips; previous year's return/assessment.

## 3. File the return on RAMIS (by 30 November)
1. Log in → **e-Services → Income Tax → Return of Income** for the Y/A.
2. Fill schedules (employment, business, investment, other), reliefs, qualifying payments.
3. Enter tax credits (APIT, WHT, instalments) with certificate numbers.
4. Review the computed balance; submit; save the acknowledgement PDF.
Senior citizens may file on paper or online.

## 4. Pay
- Online (bank/card via RAMIS payment gateway), at a bank counter with the IRD payment slip, or via internet banking. Use the correct tax type (Income Tax) and Y/A; keep the receipt.
- Final balance by 30 Sep; instalments by their due dates.

## 5. Corrections
- Revise a filed return on RAMIS where permitted (time limits apply); refunds are claimed on the return.
- Disagree with an assessment → written **objection** to the Commissioner-General within the statutory period (30 days from notice — confirm), then appeal to the Tax Appeals Commission. Refer complex cases to a registered tax advisor.
