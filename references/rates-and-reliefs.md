# Rates, reliefs and calculation method (snapshot: October 2026)

## Slabs by Year of Assessment (Y/A = 1 April → 31 March)
| Y/A | Personal relief | Bands (taxable income after relief) |
|---|---|---|
| 2026/27 | Rs. 1,800,000 | 1,000,000 @6% · next 500,000 @18% · next 500,000 @24% · next 500,000 @30% · balance @36% |
| 2025/26 | Rs. 1,800,000 | same as above (Amendment Act No. 2 of 2025) |
| 2024/25 | Rs. 1,200,000 | 5 × 500,000 bands @6/12/18/24/30% · balance @36% |
| 2023/24 | Rs. 1,200,000 | same as 2024/25 *(inferred; confirm for part-year 2022/23–Jan 2023 cases, where relief was Rs. 3M until 31 Dec 2022)* |

Never apply 2025/26 rules to 2024/25 or earlier.

## Reliefs and deductions
- **Personal relief**: residents and non-resident citizens. Rs. 150,000/month for APIT.
- **Rent relief**: 25% of gross rent in place of actual repairs/depreciation/interest claims.
- **Solar relief**: cost of solar panels (or loan repayments) up to Rs. 600,000 a year.
- **Qualifying payments**: donations to approved charities / Government — lower of Rs. 75,000 or 1/3 of taxable income (before the donation).
- **Exempt / outside the bands**: dividends (15% final WHT), approved provident-fund withdrawals, life-insurance payouts on death/maturity, gain on a personal motor vehicle sale.

## Foreign income
- Foreign-currency service exports and foreign-source income **remitted through a bank**: normal bands but no band above **15%**.
- Residents are taxed on worldwide income. Leaving for ≥1 year under a contract with an unrelated foreign employer ⇒ not resident for that contract period (from Y/A 2025/26).
- Foreign tax credit / double-tax treaty cases → general rule + refer to a registered tax advisor.
- Calculator assumption: personal relief and deductions reduce ordinary income first; the foreign income is treated as the top slice taxed at min(band rate, 15%).

## Capital gains tax (CGT) — investment assets (land, shares, etc.)
- Individuals: **10%** to 2 June 2026, **15%** from 3 June 2026. Personal relief does not apply. Payable within **30 days** of the sale. Gains are not added to the bands.
- Gain = consideration − cost (indexation not available). Confirm transitional rules for sales near 3 June 2026.

## Terminal benefits
Gratuity, commutation and compensation for loss of office have a separate concessionary table → give the general rule and refer to IRD / advisor.

## Calculation method (by hand)
1. Assessable income = employment + net business + interest + rent + other (exclude final-WHT dividends, exempt items).
2. Subtract rent relief (25% of rent), personal relief, solar relief.
3. Subtract donations (capped) → **taxable income** (floor at 0).
4. Apply bands cumulatively; foreign layer capped at 15%.
5. Subtract credits: APIT, AIT/WHT on interest, rent WHT, service-fee WHT, instalments.
6. Positive → pay by 30 September following the Y/A; negative → refund claim within 30 months.

Worked check (Y/A 2025/26): taxable Rs. 2,200,000 → 60,000 + 90,000 + 120,000 + 60,000 = **Rs. 330,000**.
