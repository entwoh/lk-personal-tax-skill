# APIT, AIT and WHT (snapshot: October 2026)

| Payment | Rate | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **APIT** (salary) | Per IRD APIT table; relief Rs. 150,000/month | Employer deducts monthly, pays to IRD by the 15th of next month, issues T-10 certificate. Tax credit on your return. |
| **AIT/WHT on interest** (FD, savings, treasury) | 10% | Credit against tax. Low-income savers can give the bank a **self-declaration** (total income below the relief) so no AIT is deducted; below-threshold rules change — confirm with the bank/IRD. |
| **WHT on rent** | 10% | Where rent paid exceeds Rs. 100,000/month. Credit on your return. |
| **WHT on service fees** (residents) | 5% | Payments above Rs. 100,000 per month by withholding agents. Credit against tax. |
| **WHT on dividends** | 15% | Final tax — not added to income. |

## Practical points
- Ask payers for the **WHT certificate** every time; without it you cannot claim the credit.
- Interest up to Rs. 5,000 for the year plus APIT-taxed salary does not trigger a return obligation.
- Credits above the tax due are refundable (claim within 30 months of the end of the Y/A).
- Rates and thresholds for WHT have shifted in recent Amendment Acts; verify current values on ird.gov.lk before quoting to a payer or payee.
